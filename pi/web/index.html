Spotify

–

The box locks to one account so no passing phone can take it over. Switch account opens it briefly: pick the box under Devices in the Spotify app (same Wi-Fi) with the account you want — it logs in, stays remembered, and re-locks on its own.

Bluetooth speaker

–

Put the speaker in pairing mode first. “Scan” lists nearby devices so you can pick one; “Pair nearest” automatically connects to the single audio device in pairing mode (~30–60 s).

Wi-Fi

–

“Save network” stores it without needing it in range — pre-add the cabin or grandparent Wi-Fi before travelling, and the box joins it by itself on arrival. Joining another network moves the box: reconnect your phone to that network and reopen this page (tapbox.local or the new IP). “Reconnect” turns the radio back on and restarts the auto-off timer — same as toggling Wi-Fi on from the box’s screen.
